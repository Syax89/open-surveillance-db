## Context

Why is this decision needed now? What problem, gap, or review finding does it
address? Reference the driving documents (execution board items, STATUS.md
gaps, legal review findings) and the constraints considered. This section
should give a reader outside the discussion enough to understand the decision
without digging through chat history.

## Decision

What was decided, as concrete numbered points. Prefer imperative, verifiable
statements ("exports must carry the ODbL notices") over descriptions of the
discussion. Record the decision owner where the decision was made above the
team (see ADR 0008, 0010, 0011 for the style).

## Consequences

What changes as a result, per owner or area where useful: implementation,
documentation, operations, review, future work. Include the trade-offs that
were accepted, not just the wins.

## Alternatives

Options that were considered and rejected, with one line each on why. A
decision without alternatives is harder to revisit later (CONTRIBUTING.md
asks for "context, alternatives, and consequences"). If no alternative was
viable, say so explicitly.
