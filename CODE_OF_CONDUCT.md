# Code of Conduct

OpenSurveillanceDB is a collaboration space for people with different experiences of privacy, safety, technology, and public space. We commit to a respectful, evidence-led, and non-hostile environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, constructive, and precise.
- Discuss systems and evidence without targeting individuals or communities.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal data or sensitive operational information.
- Welcome good-faith questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Keep criticism about ideas, implementations, and policies rather than people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Doxxing, stalking, or sharing private information without consent.
- Using project spaces to coordinate harassment, unlawful activity, or avoidance of legitimate safety controls.
- Uploading live-feed links, credentials, private camera locations, or images that expose people unnecessarily.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/contact channel (`privacy@opensurveillancedb.org`). Reports will
be handled discreetly, with proportionate action that may include content
removal, a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces.
Governance and reporting contacts are published on the `/contatti` page.
